The same rules for every extension

An extension gets access to the sites you work on. This is what ours do with it.

  • Your data stays on your computer

    Chats, ads and images are converted inside your browser and saved to your Downloads folder. We run no servers that could receive them.

  • No tracking of any kind

    No analytics, no ads, no affiliate links. Apart from the site an extension works on, it only asks the payment service whether Pro is unlocked.

  • Free to start, fair to upgrade

    Every extension has a free plan with no time limit. Pro is paid monthly or once, checkout runs through Stripe, and a full refund within 14 days is yours if you ask.

Who makes these: Tabfinch, an independent developer. When a website changes and something stops working, a fix goes out as soon as the store approves it. Questions and bug reports: the support page. The details are in the privacy policy and the terms.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Install an extension and use its free plan straight away. You only type an e-mail address if you buy Pro, and you do that on the payment service’s own page.

How do I buy Pro?

From inside the extension. Its upgrade button opens the checkout of ExtensionPay, which takes the payment through Stripe, and Pro unlocks in that browser right after you pay. Your card details go to Stripe only.

Can I pay once instead of every month?

Yes. DocShift and AdPluck can be paid monthly or once; PicShift is a one-time purchase only. A one-time purchase includes future updates.

How do I cancel a monthly plan?

In DocShift and AdPluck, open the extension’s options and choose “Manage or cancel your plan”. Pro stays on until the end of the month you have already paid for.

Can I get my money back?

Yes, within 14 days of buying, no questions asked. Reply to the receipt you were e-mailed, or write to the support page. The terms have the details.

Can I use Pro on another computer?

Yes, on your own devices. In the extension choose “Already paid? Restore your purchase” and enter the e-mail address you paid with.

Which browsers do they work in?

Desktop Chrome, Edge and Brave, from the Chrome Web Store; Firefox versions are on the way. The page of each extension shows where you can install it today.
