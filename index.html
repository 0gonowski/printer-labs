Small browser extensions that do one job well

No accounts, no tracking, nothing uploaded. Free for everyday use, with a Pro upgrade for people who use them at volume.

DocShift

Turns a ChatGPT or Gemini conversation into a real Word document. Equations arrive as native Word equations you can click and edit, tables stay tables, and code keeps its spacing.

  • Word, PDF, Markdown, HTML and plain text
  • On ChatGPT it reads the conversation data rather than the page layout, so a site redesign does not break it
  • One extension for both sites. Free: 5 exports a week in full quality. Pro: unlimited, $2.99 a month or $19.99 once
Add DocShift to Chrome

AdPluck

Adds what Meta Ad Library is missing: a download button on every ad, a badge that shows how long it has been running, filters, and export to CSV.

  • Images and videos in the best available quality, carousels included
  • A private swipe file stored in your browser
  • Free: unlimited single downloads. Pro: bulk download and full exports, $4.99 a month or $29 once
Add AdPluck to Chrome

PicShift

Right-click any image and save it as PNG, JPG or WebP. It converts WebP and AVIF files on your own computer, so the picture opens in the app you actually use.

  • Copy an image as PNG, ready to paste
  • Nothing is uploaded, and the code is not obfuscated
  • Free: unlimited single images. Pro: whole pages at once, ZIP and resize, $6.99 once
