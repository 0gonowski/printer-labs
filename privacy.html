Privacy policy

Our extensions do their work inside your browser. We run no servers that receive your data, and neither the extensions nor this website contain analytics, tracking, ads or cookies.

Effective date: 28 November 2026. Applies to DocShift, AdPluck, PicShift and BubbleBoost, and to this website. Polska wersja

Who is responsible for your data

The extensions and this website are run by Tabfinch (“we”). For anything about your data, contact us through the support page.

What the extensions collect

Nothing about how you use them. The extensions do not send us page content, browsing history, files, usage statistics or identifiers, and we have no way to see what you do with them. The only personal data we receive is what you give when you buy Pro or start a trial, described below, and what you write to us.

Settings, saved items, the progress of a run and the free-plan counters are kept in your browser’s extension storage on your device. Uninstalling an extension deletes them.

The one service they talk to: checking for Pro

Pro is sold through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com). For each extension, ExtensionPay issues a random key for your browser, and the extension asks ExtensionPay whether that key has Pro, or for BubbleBoost a trial. The key says nothing about you or the pages you visit, and nothing else is sent.

DocShift, AdPluck and PicShift get that key only when you open the Pro checkout; until then they contact no one but the site they work on, and afterwards they ask at most a few times a day. BubbleBoost’s copy of ExtensionPay’s library creates the key when it first checks your plan, and keeps the key and ExtensionPay’s last answer in your browser’s extension storage, which your browser may sync between your own devices if you turned sync on.

Buying Pro and the BubbleBoost trial

Payments are processed by Stripe through ExtensionPay. Your card details go to Stripe and are never seen by us or by the extension. To buy Pro, or to start BubbleBoost’s free trial, you enter your e-mail address on ExtensionPay’s own page. From ExtensionPay and Stripe we receive your e-mail address, the name and country you give at checkout, and payment details such as the amount, the date and the last four digits of the card. Their own privacy policies apply to their pages: ExtensionPay, Stripe.

Who receives it

  • ExtensionPay, which sells Pro for us and tells the extension whether it is unlocked (United States).
  • Stripe, which processes the payment (Ireland and the United States).
  • GitHub, which hosts this website and our support page (United States).
  • Our e-mail provider, when you write to us.
  • Public authorities, such as the tax office, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

Transfers outside the European Union

ExtensionPay, Stripe and GitHub process data in the United States. Stripe and GitHub are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, so their transfers rely on the European Commission’s adequacy decision of 10 July 2023. The transfer to ExtensionPay is necessary to perform the contract you ask for (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR).

This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, loads nothing from other websites and has no analytics, which is why it does not ask for cookie consent. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads, and its privacy statement says it may keep logs of visitors’ IP addresses to keep its service secure; GitHub decides on those logs itself.

Store buttons take you to the store’s own page, where the store’s privacy policy applies; their address carries a tag naming the page of this site you came from, which says nothing about you. “E-mail me the link” opens your own e-mail app with the page’s address, and nothing passes through us. Reports sent from “Something not working?” are posted on GitHub and are public there.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its use, to receive a copy of it and to object to its use based on our legitimate interest. Write to the support page; we reply within one month. For data that ExtensionPay or Stripe hold about your purchase, you can write to us and we will pass the request on, or contact them directly.

You can also complain to a data protection authority in the EU country where you live or work.

Giving your e-mail address is voluntary, but without it you cannot buy Pro or start the trial. We make no automated decisions about you and do no profiling.

Permissions

Each extension asks only for access to the sites it works on, plus the browser features its job needs, such as storage and downloads. PicShift is the exception: it can read images on any site, because that is the only way to fetch and convert an image you right-click. It reads an image only when you ask it to.

When you remove an extension

Removing DocShift, AdPluck or PicShift opens a page on this site with quick fixes and a way to report a problem. Its address carries only the name of the extension, and the page sends nothing unless you choose to report a problem.

Children

The extensions are general-purpose tools and are not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: the support page.
