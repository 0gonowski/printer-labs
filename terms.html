Terms and refunds

Short version: the free plan is free for good, Pro is paid monthly or once, whichever you choose, and you can have your money back within 14 days.

Effective date: 28 November 2026. Applies to DocShift, AdPluck, PicShift and BubbleBoost, and to this website. Polska wersja: Regulamin

Who sells what

The extensions are made and sold by Tabfinch (“we”).

Google, Microsoft and Mozilla distribute them through their stores but are not the seller. Payments for Pro are processed by Stripe through ExtensionPay.

What you need

A computer with an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Brave or other Chromium-based browser, or Firefox where an extension is offered for it; an internet connection; and your own access to the websites an extension works on, such as a ChatGPT or Gemini account for DocShift.

Free and Pro

Each extension states its free limits in its store listing, on this site and in the extension itself. Pro removes those limits and adds the features listed there. Pro is bought separately for each extension, either as a monthly plan or as a one-time purchase with updates included; the checkout shows which options an extension has. BubbleBoost also offers a free 7-day trial of Pro, which needs only an e-mail address and does not renew.

Buying Pro

You choose a plan in the extension, which opens ExtensionPay’s checkout page, where Stripe takes the payment. Prices are in US dollars and are the total you pay; your bank may add a currency conversion fee. The contract is made when the payment goes through: Pro unlocks in the extension, and you get a receipt by e-mail.

A monthly plan renews each month until you cancel it, which you can do at any time from the extension: “Manage or cancel your plan” on the options page of DocShift and AdPluck, or “Manage subscription” in BubbleBoost’s popup. Pro then stays on until the end of the month already paid for.

Your license

  • Pro is for one person. To use it in another browser of yours, choose “Already paid? Restore your purchase” in the extension (in BubbleBoost: “Already have Pro? Log in”) and sign in with the e-mail address you paid with.
  • Please do not share access. Access that is shared publicly may be switched off.
  • The extensions check with the payment service from time to time. DocShift, AdPluck and PicShift keep Pro working for seven days when you are offline.

Refunds and withdrawal

You can withdraw from a purchase within 14 days of buying, without giving a reason, and get a full refund. If you are a consumer in the EU or the UK, this is also your statutory right of withdrawal. To withdraw, tell us before the 14 days are over: reply to the receipt you were e-mailed when you paid, or write to the support page. You may use the model form below, but you do not have to: the e-mail address you paid with is enough for us to find the purchase.

We confirm by e-mail that we have received your withdrawal and refund the full amount within 14 days of receiving it, to the card you paid with, at no cost to you. Pro is switched off once the refund is made. Please do not post payment details on a public page.

If something does not work

If an extension does not work as described, write to the support page with the name of the extension, what went wrong and the e-mail address you paid with. We reply within 14 days. As a consumer you have the statutory rights for digital content that does not conform to the contract: we fix the problem first, and if we cannot, you can have the price reduced or end the contract and get your money back.

We provide the updates needed to keep Pro working as described: for a monthly plan while it lasts, and for a one-time purchase for as long as we keep the extension in the stores. The websites the extensions work on can change at any time, and a feature may stop working until we update the extension; we fix such breakages as quickly as we can.

Consumers in the EU can get free help from the European Consumer Centre in their country.

Using the extensions responsibly

  • The extensions work on other companies’ websites. Those sites have their own terms, and following them is up to you.
  • BubbleBoost can favorite designs on Redbubble automatically. Redbubble’s rules restrict bots and automated tools, and accounts that break them can be suspended; use that feature in moderation and at your own risk.
  • Ads, images and chat content you save may be protected by copyright or contain other people’s data. You are responsible for what you do with what you save.

Liability

If you are a consumer, the statutory rules on liability apply in full, and nothing in these terms limits them. If you buy as a business, the software is provided as is: to the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, and our total liability is limited to what you paid for the license.

This website

This website gives free information about our extensions. You need only a current web browser, and you stop using it simply by leaving. It sets no cookies and runs no code from other websites. Please do not send unlawful content in problem reports. As anywhere online, install extensions only from the official stores and be careful with links and files from unknown sources. Comments about the website go to the support page; we reply within 14 days.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date and applies to purchases made after that date. If a change affects a monthly plan you already have, we e-mail you at least 14 days before it takes effect, and you can cancel before then.

Law and disputes

If you are a consumer, you keep the protection of the mandatory laws of the country where you live, and you can bring a claim before the courts there.
