Who sells what

The extensions are made and sold by Printer Labs. Google, Microsoft and Mozilla distribute them through their stores but are not the seller. Payments for Pro are processed by Stripe through ExtensionPay.

Free and Pro

Each extension states its free limits in its store listing and in the extension itself. Pro removes those limits and adds the features listed there. Pro is bought separately for each extension, either as a monthly plan or as a one-time purchase with updates included; the checkout shows which options an extension has. A monthly plan renews each month until you cancel it, which you can do at any time from the extension's options page ("Manage or cancel your plan"); Pro then stays on until the end of the month already paid for.

Your license

  • Pro is for one person. To use it in another browser of yours, choose "Restore your purchase" in the extension and sign in with the email address you paid with.
  • Please do not share access. Access that is shared publicly may be switched off.
  • The extension checks with the payment service about once a day. If you are offline, Pro keeps working for seven days.

Refunds

If Pro is not what you needed, reply to the receipt you were emailed when you paid, or contact us through the support page, within 14 days of buying, and you will get a full refund, no questions asked. Pro is switched off once the refund is made. Please do not post payment details on a public page; the email address you paid with is enough.
