How to download videos and images from Meta (Facebook) Ad Library

Meta Ad Library shows every active ad on Facebook and Instagram, but it has no download button. Here is how to save an ad's creative by hand, and how to do it in one click.

Updated 27 November 2026. Written by Printer Labs, the developer of AdPluck, the second method below.

1. By hand, with the browser's developer tools

  1. Open the ad in Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) and start the video once.
  2. Press F12 to open the developer tools and choose the Network tab, then the Media filter.
  3. Play the video again; a request for an .mp4 file appears. Open it in a new tab.
  4. Right-click the video in that tab and choose Save video as…

It works, but it is fiddly: the address changes, you may catch a low-quality version, and a carousel means repeating it for every card. Images are easier: right-click the image and save it, though the file name tells you nothing about the ad.

2. In one click, with AdPluck

AdPluck adds a Download button to every ad in Meta Ad Library:

  • images and videos in the best quality the library has, carousels included;
  • files named after the advertiser and the ad's library ID, so you can find them later;
  • a "days running" badge on every ad, with ads running 30 days or more highlighted;
  • one-click copy of the ad text, and export of the loaded ads to CSV or JSON.

Questions

Can I reuse the ads I download?

The creatives belong to the advertisers. Use what you save for research and inspiration, not for re-uploading someone else's work.

Do I need to be logged in to Facebook?

No. Meta Ad Library is public, and AdPluck works on it without logging in.

Does AdPluck do anything on the rest of Facebook?

No. It works only on Meta Ad Library pages, and everything you save stays on your computer.
