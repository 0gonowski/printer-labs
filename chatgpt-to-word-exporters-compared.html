Which one fits

  • You hand in Word documents with math (coursework, lab reports, derivations): DocShift is built for that case; every formula becomes a Word equation you can edit, and the free allowance resets every week rather than every month.
  • You want PDF or Markdown of ChatGPT chats: ChatGPT Exporter is the most used and best rated of the group.
  • You need several chat sites today, including Claude: AI Exporter covers ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.
  • You mostly need Word or PDF without heavy math: ChatGPT to Word or PDF is widely used; check whether its monthly allowance suits how often you export.

What to check before you install any of them

  • Whether the chat is converted in your browser or uploaded to the developer's server; the store page's privacy section says which.
  • Whether the free allowance is per week or per month, and whether it covers the format you need.
  • Recent reviews: chat sites change often, and a review from last week says more than the average rating.
