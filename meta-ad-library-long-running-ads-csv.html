How to find long-running ads in Meta Ad Library and export them to CSV

Meta Ad Library does not show spend or results for ordinary ads, but it shows when each ad started. An ad that keeps running is usually an ad that makes money, so the start date is the best free signal you have. Here is how to use it, and how to get the ads into a spreadsheet.

Updated 27 November 2026. By Tabfinch, the maker of AdPluck, which the second half of this page uses.

Find the ads that keep running

  1. Open Meta Ad Library, pick a country and All ads, and search for a competitor or a phrase from your niche. Put a phrase in quotes for exact matches.
  2. Keep Active ads selected: an ad still running after weeks is the interesting kind.
  3. Read the Started running on date on each card. Ads live for 30 days or more go on your shortlist; new ones show what the advertiser is testing.
  4. Open ads marked multiple versions: comparing the versions shows which hooks and visuals they are testing.
  5. Click through to the landing page. The offer behind an ad is often what makes it work.

By hand this means doing date arithmetic on every card and copying details into a sheet one ad at a time.

Questions

Is days running the same as profitable?

Not always: some advertisers forget ads, and big brands keep awareness ads running. Treat it as a shortlist, then judge the creative and the offer.

Where does the data go?

Nowhere but your computer. AdPluck reads the ads the library already shows you, and the CSV is saved to your Downloads folder.
