Download Meta Ad Library ads in HD, and spot the ones that keep running

AdPluck adds what the library is missing: a download button on every ad, a badge with the days each ad has been live, filters, and an export to CSV. For media buyers, founders and agencies who study competitors’ ads every week.

How it works

  1. Search the library

    Open Meta Ad Library and search for a keyword or an advertiser, as you do now.

  2. Use the buttons on each ad

    Download the image or video, copy the ad text, or save the ad to your swipe file.

  3. Open the AdPluck panel

    Filter by days running, export to CSV or JSON, or download everything at once.

See which ads keep running

Every ad gets a badge with the number of days it has been live, and ads past 30 days stand out. An ad that keeps running is usually an ad that makes money.

  • Filter by minimum days running
  • Show images or videos only
  • Hide duplicate creatives

Keep a private swipe file

Save an ad with its thumbnail and your own notes, and search your collection later. It is kept in your browser, not on a server.

  • The free plan holds 25 ads; Pro has no limit
  • Thumbnails stay visible after the original media link expires

Export what you found to a spreadsheet

Advertiser, start date, days running, platforms, ad text, headline, call to action, landing page and media links, as CSV or JSON.

  • The free plan exports the first 25 loaded ads
  • Pro exports every one, and “Load more ads” scrolls the results for you, up to 1,000 ads

Questions about AdPluck

Do I need a Facebook account?

No. Meta Ad Library is public, and AdPluck works on the library pages you open. It never logs in for you.

Does it work on the rest of Facebook?

No. It works on Meta Ad Library pages only (facebook.com/ads/library) and does nothing anywhere else.

What quality are the downloads?

The best the library has for that ad: images and videos in HD where available, carousels included. Files are named after the advertiser and the library ID.

Can I reuse the ads I download?

Ad creatives belong to their owners. Use what you save for research and inspiration, not to re-upload someone else’s work.

Where is my swipe file stored?

In your browser’s storage on your computer, not on a server. Removing AdPluck deletes it.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. Pro is $4.99 a month or $29 once. Either way you can ask for a full refund within 14 days.
