What happened to “Save image as Type”, and what to use instead

For years “Save image as Type” was the usual way to save web images as PNG or JPG in Chrome. In March 2026 Google removed it from the Chrome Web Store and switched it off in Chrome. Here is what was reported, how to check your browser, and the options you have now.

Updated 27 November 2026. By Tabfinch, the maker of PicShift, one of the options below.

What was reported

According to 9to5Google (16 March 2026), the extension, which had at least a million users, changed hands in late 2025. A script inside it then contacted a remote server and added affiliate codes to links on hundreds of shopping sites, so commissions from users' purchases went to someone else. Google flagged the extension as malware, removed it from the Chrome Web Store and disabled it in Chrome.

A tool that is useful for years and is then sold to a new owner who adds hidden monetization is a known way for popular extensions to turn bad. It is worth keeping in mind when you choose a replacement.

Check your browser

  1. Open chrome://extensions.
  2. If “Save image as Type” is still listed, Chrome will have turned it off and marked it. Click Remove.
  3. While you are there, remove extensions you no longer use, especially those allowed to read and change data on all websites.

What a trustworthy image-saving extension looks like

Saving an image in another format means fetching the image and converting it, and the picture you right-click is often hosted on a different domain than the page. So an extension that does this job needs permission to read images on all sites; that permission alone is not a warning sign. What matters is what the extension does with it:

  • It converts images on your computer and does not upload them.
  • It makes no other network requests: no ads, no affiliate links, no analytics.
  • Its code is readable, not minified or obfuscated, so reviewers and curious users can check it.
  • Its store page names the publisher, a privacy policy and a way to get support.

Your options now

No extension: convert after saving

Save the image as it is, open it in Paint or Photos on Windows or in Preview on a Mac, and use Save as or Export to choose PNG or JPEG. Fine for an image now and then.

Online converters

They work in any browser, but the image is uploaded to someone else's server. Keep personal or confidential pictures out of them.

Save Image As PNG

The extension 9to5Google pointed to as an alternative in its report.

PicShift (ours)

Right-click any image, open the PicShift menu and choose PNG, JPG or WebP. The image is converted in your browser and never uploaded. It opens WebP, AVIF, PNG, JPG, GIF, SVG, BMP and ICO and keeps the original pixel size. There are no ads, analytics or affiliate links, and the code is not obfuscated. Single images are free with no limit; a one-time Pro upgrade ($6.99) adds converting every image on a page at once, ZIP files and resizing.
