Copy Redbubble tags in one click, and see the top tags of any search

The top tags of any search or shop

Press “Top tags” on a search, tag or artist shop page to see which tags its designs use most, with a count for each. BubbleBoost reads them from the page’s own data, so it works on every language version of Redbubble.

  • Free: the 10 most used tags
  • Pro: every tag, ready to pick and copy, or the top 15 at once
  • Pro: the page’s price range and average price

Favorite designs automatically, at your own pace

Many sellers favorite other artists’ newest work, hoping some of them notice and visit their shop in return. Press Start on a search or shop page and BubbleBoost takes the clicking off your hands, one design at a time, then moves on to the next page.

  • Counts only favorites Redbubble confirms, and skips designs already in your lists
  • A random pause of 0.5 to 3 seconds between favorites
  • “Stop after” ends a session after 50, 100, 200 or 500 favorites

Use automatic favorites in moderation. Redbubble’s rules restrict bots and automated tools, and accounts that break them can be suspended. Favorites do not promise sales: what happens next is up to the people who receive them.

Questions about BubbleBoost

Can my Redbubble account get suspended?

Redbubble’s rules restrict bots and automated tools, and accounts that break them can be suspended, so use automatic favorites in moderation and at your own risk. Copy tags and Top tags only read the page you are on.

Will favorites bring me sales?

Not by themselves. Favorites can make other artists notice your shop; what they do next is up to them.

How does the free trial work?

Enter your e-mail address on ExtensionPay’s page and confirm it. You get every Pro feature for 7 days, with no card, and nothing renews.

I already pay for BubbleBoost. Does anything change?

No. Your plan and price stay exactly as they are, and the new tag tools are included. On another computer, choose “Already have Pro? Log in” in the popup.

Does it work on Redbubble in other languages?

Yes. It reads the tags from the page’s data rather than the visible text, so every language version of the site works.
