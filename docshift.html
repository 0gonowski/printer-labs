Questions about DocShift

Are the equations pictures?

No. Each formula becomes a native Word equation. Double-click it in Word to edit it, as if you had typed it there.

What happens after 5 exports in a week?

The export window tells you, and the count starts again on Monday. Pro removes the limit.

Is my conversation uploaded anywhere?

No. It is converted in your browser and saved on your computer. On ChatGPT, DocShift asks chatgpt.com for the open conversation with the session you are already signed in with; nothing goes to us.

Can I open the file in Google Docs?

Yes. Open the .docx in Google Docs and the equations come across as Google Docs equations.

Does the PDF keep the formulas sharp?

Yes. The formulas are text, not pictures, whether you export a PDF from DocShift or save the Word file as PDF.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. Pro is $2.99 a month or $19.99 once. Either way you can ask for a full refund within 14 days.
